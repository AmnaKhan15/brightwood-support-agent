# Returns

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in your order, not the order date.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Items marked final sale, including clearance items and gift cards, cannot be returned.

## How to return
Ask for a return label. The label is free and prepaid. Print it, attach it to the parcel and drop it at any carrier location. The label is valid for 14 days.
