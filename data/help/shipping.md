# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders ship within 1 business day of being placed.

## Shipping costs
Shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars. Otherwise standard shipping costs 6 dollars and express costs 15 dollars.

## International orders
We currently ship only within the country of the store. We do not ship internationally.
