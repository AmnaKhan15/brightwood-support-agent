# Refunds

## When refunds are issued
A refund goes back to the original payment method. It arrives 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item.

## Refund limits for the assistant
The support assistant can issue a refund of up to 50 dollars on its own. A refund above 50 dollars needs approval from a support manager. Only one refund can be made per order.

## What is refunded
The item price is refunded. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or the mistake was ours.
